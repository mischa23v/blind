# Traversability loss: preserved aggregate findings

**Stock Voxblox produced long projected detours, not a repaired short aisle.** Twelve of twenty ordered pairs have complete projected corridors, but these are **16.995–17.365 m** for reference-model routes of **2.000–2.250 m**. They represent six unordered pairs in both directions, sharing much of one detour—not twelve independent journeys. No physical, strict-endpoint, requested-connector or full-footprint-support admission was granted.

This is a **text-only preservation packet** of completed diagnostics and saved-data audits. It ran no mapper, native planner, geometry query, new scoring or experiment. Root alone publishes. `PUBLIC_MANIFEST.json` is the exact file whitelist. No source activation, dataset redistribution rights, new run authorization or safety claim follows.

## Correction: `sourceFloorMissingRays` does not mean absent geometry

The old immutable output counted **25 Open3D and 11 stock rays** under `sourceFloorMissingRays`. The saved audit found source hits inside the diagnostic height band for **all 36**; they failed only the `absNormalWorldZ >= 0.9` selector. Read the metric as **“no source floor candidate under this band-and-normal selector”**, not no geometry, missing physical floor or clearance. Original receipts remain intact; their misleading label is corrected here, not silently rewritten.

A different reporting metric—no floor-band raster span—covered 22 unique Open3D columns and 14 stock columns. Each omitted column contained one overhead span and no lower raster span; a slightly shifted lower floor was not merely hidden just outside the reporting band. This is absence of lower *raster geometry*, not proof of a physical hole or absent real support. The measured local floor-height discrepancy must not be generalized into a room-wide correction.

## Object fidelity remains unresolved

At six unchanged witness positions, 678 footprint rays contain 24 reference-positive surface intersections. **Neither map has a same-ray source match for any of the 24** within the frozen descriptive ±4 cm height band. This is not a calibrated physical tolerance or proof that entire objects disappeared.

Stock's twenty “raised body-band” rays contain 25 near-horizontal source-floor hits: **5.339–6.134 cm above the saved reference floor**, but only **0–2.192×10⁻⁸ m above their own source-floor candidate**. They are floor-height discrepancies, not recovered furniture. These witnesses lie on the old reference/diagnostic feet, not the newly generated detours. Nearest-surface distance alone is not object identity or retained-obstacle proof.

## Saved erosion-seed audit

Saved-span replay exactly matched all ledge/headroom outputs for **96,274 Open3D / 92,946 stock raw spans** and all erosion outputs for **28,740 / 29,704 compact spans**. Float32 quantization gives height **61 voxels**, climb **2**, radius **6**, erosion threshold **12**; nominal 1.2 m height becomes about 1.22 m in this raster. No parameters were changed. Replay used 1.943 CPU / 1.945 wall seconds and 108,793,856 B maximum RSS.

The selected ancestry is **deterministic, source-matched saved-span replay**, not recorded native distance/seed provenance, a unique physically nearest obstacle, or continuous physical clearance. Area-result parity alone cannot verify native distance bytes or tie-selected seed identity. Generic boundary/MAX_LAYERS branches omitted from the replay were inactive in these saved inputs; this is not a general replacement implementation. Saved source-ray facets support some initial slope rejections, but are not exhaustive raster contributors and do not exclude area-merge effects. Stock contributor identities remain unresolved.
