# Frozen phone-only compact map protocol v1

Scope: offline estimated game geometry from the 294 pose-matched processed Koivu iPhone depth exports. This is not raw sensor replay, physical clearance, measured gravity, localization, or a production `Observation`. Original confidence, tracking, capture clock and body mount remain unavailable. The separate FARO mesh/alignment is forbidden to the builder. All routes are selected only after this map is frozen. No tuning to FARO outcomes.

## Inputs and bounds

- Exact all-pose frame list: `DEPTH_SELECTION.json`; 294 files / 67,180,136 bytes. The exclusive extracted `depths/DEPTH_INPUT_MANIFEST.json` pins every original PNG. Original pose file SHA256 `c496c8dc6474679766db8abd92da9c756104a752bb9e4519fd462fe294af90c3`.
- Decode original UInt16 PNG to float64 nominal metres at 0.001. Every nonzero pixel is an endpoint candidate (0.001–65.535 m); zero remains missing. No stride, confidence manufacture, interpolation, or depth smoothing. For estimated free coverage only, measured axial depth must also be in [0.15,8] m; this is an experiment bound, not native confidence.
- Reuse source-verified half-pixel OpenGL projection: `[(col+.5-cx)/fx, -(row+.5-cy)/fy, -1] * depth`, then exact per-frame camera-to-phone matrix. Check matrix and calibration before use.
- Initial phone-world ROI is the full camera-position AABB expanded by 3 m horizontally and 3 m vertically, rounded outward to 0.05 m. Bounds are map limits, not clearance. Every finite positive endpoint outside ROI is counted separately, never treated as free. Unknown space outside map is blocked.
- Positive occupancy resolution is 0.05 m; voxel origin is the lower ROI bound rounded down to a 0.05 m multiple. No positive hit is removed by later free evidence. This retains every accepted input endpoint within ROI but does **not** prove every physical thin/low object was measured or preserved.

## Phone-only level model

- Phone export +Z is the initial game-up assumption, not measured gravity. Select the horizontal-height bin with most distinct occupied 5 cm XY cells in the range [median cameraZ−2.5, median cameraZ−0.5] m; ties choose the lowest bin. No FARO/source RGB labels influence this choice.
- Fit `z=a*x+b*y+c` with NumPy least squares to occupied voxel centres within 0.075 m of that selected height. Perform exactly three total deterministic fit-then-trim iterations retaining residual <=0.03 m, never reintroducing rejected points. Each design must have rank3, finite coefficients, and >=200 retained points with both horizontal spans >=1m after each trim. Require >=200 distinct points, horizontal spans >=1 m on both axes, and tilt <=5 degrees from assumed +Z, otherwise map construction fails without fallback/resampling.
- Create an orthonormal app frame with +Y the fitted upward normal, +X the normalized projection of phone +X onto the plane, +Z=+X cross +Y, and floor plane Y=0. Export exact phone-to-app/app-to-phone transforms. The evaluator may apply these transforms, but cannot alter the planner map.
- Second pass retains every in-ROI original endpoint in 5 cm app-frame voxels, with actual minimum/maximum Y, hit count and number of observing frames. Ground-band classification is only the declared model tolerance [-0.03,+0.03] m. All voxels, including ground-band hits, remain in the artifact. Any hit extending above +0.03 and below 1.75 m is positive body-height blockage; a voxel with minimum observedY <−0.03 and maximum observedY >−0.25m is a below-floor contradiction and revokes support. The strict interval overlap applies even to a mixed ground/obstacle voxel; a voxel with any observedY above+0.03 and any below1.75m blocks that horizontal cell. No 9 cm exclusion.
- This 3 cm floor band is an unresolved small-height ambiguity, not a claim to preserve hazards within it. Independent detailed-reference scoring is expected to expose misses. No threshold changes after map output or collision results.

## Surface/coverage model

- Surface raster resolution 0.10 m. App horizontal bounds enclose the transformed initial ROI; origin rounded down to 0.10 m. Floor=0 only for candidate cells with all four 5 cm horizontal quadrants containing positive floor-band support, each observed in at least two distinct frames. Residual/min/max/extents retained. Otherwise floor=null.
- Floor model is explicitly level; `floorUncertainty=null` means unavailable, never claimed zero. Native `NavigationSurface` receives a **simulation-only** SurfaceCell import. This map cannot mint production physical authority.
- Estimated vertical coverage samples five horizontal locations per 10 cm cell: centre and four corners inset 0.005 m; at 18 fixed heights 0.05,0.15,...,1.75 m. Inverse projection is u=fx*x/(−z)+cx−0.5 and v=−fy*y/(−z)+cy−0.5, with source integer column/row=floor(u+0.5),floor(v+0.5), i.e. nearest pixel-centre with positive tie. A sample is covered only if at least one actual source frame projects it inside the raster (complete 3×3 neighbourhood), its camera axial depth >=0.15 m, and the minimum of that actual 3×3 depth neighbourhood lies within [0.15,8] m and is at least sample axial depth+0.05 m. Neighbourhood minimum is conservative against local foreground edges, not invented missing-depth fill. Each invalid/zero sample fails that observation. Every one of the90 samples must have coverage for `clearance=1.75`; otherwise clearance=null.
- Occupied endpoints always override sampled free coverage. No TSDF averaging, removal by absence, custom raycaster, custom search, or FARO data. Finite sample coverage does not establish continuous full-body free volume; it is a deliberately bounded game estimate tested against independent reference geometry.
- `blocked` true if positive obstacle/floor contradiction touches the cell or support absent; unknown clearance remains null. Full endpoint boxes remain available separately. Null clearance is ineligible for both Recast and travel, not merely nullable in storage. Recast uses only eligible surface support triangles and retained obstacle containers; existing body/footprint guards apply before translation.

## Frozen outputs / compute

- `MAP.npz`: endpoint voxel indices/extents/counts; SurfaceCell floor/support/blocked/coverage arrays; transformations; no RGB/fake metadata.
- `SURFACE.json`: level, origin{x,y,z}, resolution,width,depth,cells[{floor,clearance,blocked,floorUncertainty:null}]. Receipt includes input/source hashes, selected floor model, counts/rejections, all approximations and timing.
- Serial NumPy/SciPy/Pillow only, installed components; no downloads/builds/installs. <=600 CPU /900 wall seconds, <=2 GiB observed RSS, <=64 MiB new map outputs excluding the already authorized67.2 MB original PNG extraction; maintain768 MiB disk reserve. No FARO mesh loaded concurrently. Stop and preserve failure if a bound or prerequisite fails; no favourable subset fallback.
- Fixtures before recorded construction: half-pixel projection, frame transform, all-pixel low/overhead hit retention, floor-band boundary, occupancy-over-free, zero depth/missing coverage, finite-sample coverage, exact SurfaceCell unknowns. One independent source review suffices. Map and all frame hashes freeze before route selection; no game launch until the maintainer-reviewed 100-run protocol.
