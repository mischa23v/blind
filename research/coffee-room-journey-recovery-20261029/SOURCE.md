## Original provenance and what the data measures

[Xuqian Ren et al., MuSHRoom iPhone Basic Data](https://zenodo.org/records/10230733), CC BY 4.0; [official project](https://github.com/TUTvision/MuSHRoom). Saved official acquisition receipt reports `coffee_room_iphone.tar.gz`, 183,080,957 bytes, MD5 `cb467b3ad18d8f2c83dbf97a486d378b`, SHA256 `591672a1c4afa50e5109fc78ff08d8f608d05756a9181cfdd7cef38a57a1623d`. This audit uses saved official metadata; it does not claim a fresh remote archive check.

Original Polycam mesh: 51,965 vertices / 87,370 triangles, unchanged. Original 388 poses/intrinsics are recorded camera information, **not this app localizing a person**. The official mesh is not aligned to the camera poses. The preserved readiness audit explicitly requires Coffee-specific source-only rigid registration and reserved-view checks; no Koivu transform or reference-fit substitute. Mesh bounds / candidate Y-up floor layer are not verified room dimensions or full walkable floor. The historical native topology experiment used nine authored source-bound fractions, not camera/body/seat/door observations: 30 native-complete queries, 20 association-valid proposals among endpoints 0,1,6,7,8, ten complete-but-association-rejected, 42 incomplete/invalid. Zero movement episodes.

Original RGB/depth, mesh texture/material were inventoried but not in that selected packet. The later W6 run had raw data on its prior executor; that does not establish availability after reset. The old reference-column masks are not full triangle geometry. [Coffee reference source](https://zenodo.org/records/10222321) archive `coffee_room_mesh_pd.tar.gz` is recorded as 288,148,502 bytes, MD5 `d051bb90179d53374c1bb0c6ec4b0745`; original `icp_iphone.json` and coordinate contract are needed for evaluator-only reference comparisons. FARO is processed reference with uncertain registration/completion, not perfect ground truth.

## Door and posture coverage verdict

**Not demonstrated as a complete crossing journey.** The later24-frame inspection finds a closed gray door under green exit signage, but no observed far side; details below. No recovered route endpoint is independently identified as a seat, stand point, doorway threshold, beyond-door goal or return seat. No metrically verified Coffee door jamb, crossing trajectory or exterior/corridor-side coverage was found. VARPU 130→160→130 is a different Koivu room and cannot supply Coffee exit coverage. The source-only topology audit explicitly refuses entrance/closed-wall inference. The dataset name also does not prove a public cafe or permission for a customer journey. A complete exit/return test may require additional public data with both sides observed, or a consented on-device scan; do not invent the unseen side.
