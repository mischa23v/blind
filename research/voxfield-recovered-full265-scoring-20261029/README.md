## Reproduction boundary

The included path-only score adapters preserve original math but depend on original local evaluator modules, pinned heldout intrinsics/poses, source meshes and immutable reference caches. They also require actual root-admitted mesh/recovery records and reviewed dependency/runtime paths. Those assets are not bundled; a checkout alone is not runnable. No new download, budget, raw-data publication or score tuning is authorized by this checkpoint. Reproduction must reconstruct lawful exact inputs and independently review its own freeze/admission/one-worker resource controls. Prior audit and recovery checkpoints document underlying source licences and protocol history; third-party/data terms remain separate.
