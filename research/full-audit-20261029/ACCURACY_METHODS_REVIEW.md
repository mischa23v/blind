# Accuracy methods: falsifiable improvements, not another mapper popularity list

Research-only, 2026-10-29. Read primary papers, official released source and platform documentation. **No code changed in Blind, no installation/build/test/model download, no benchmark rerun, no dependency adoption or pin claimed.** Ranked recommendations below are hypotheses about the supplied failures, not measured gains in this project. Complementary architecture/API review: `../robotics-improvement-review-20261029/README.md`.

## Decision for the full audit

Prioritize **sensor/calibration correctness and uncertainty-aware fusion**, then **normal-aware non-projective fusion**, while the existing pose-refinement A/B finishes. Add a **separate measured support/obstacle layer** and **targeted reacquisition**, rather than smoothing a map until routes appear. Temporal foreground separation is a later controlled experiment. Keep exact conservative clearance as a hard downstream invariant for every variant.

There is no evidence that swapping a library alone produces consistent “90+” whole-system accuracy. Supplied current evidence says Voxblox's 265-frame first-hit result is mixed: pooled median/MAE improved versus fixed Open3D, but within-4 m MAE/P95 worsened. Full Open3D registration A/B remains another investigation's work. Existing exact-EDT fixed-scene diagnostic removed two corner margin contacts; it is not a live-navigation pass. No number in the papers below should replace those local results.

### Common protocol (required before any proposed run)

- Freeze the exact frame IDs/order, raw files/checksums, timestamp association, RGB/depth intrinsics and resolutions, camera-to-world convention, gravity, range definition, depth validity rules, mapping bounds, voxel size, truncation, integration budget, route endpoints and collision oracle. Keep separate manifests for each variant.
- Verify axial camera-z depth versus Euclidean ray range, depth-unit scaling, resized intrinsics, pixel-center conventions, rotations and transform direction analytically on test points. Do not repair a sensor-contract bug by adjusting map parameters.
- Map from sensor frames and estimated/device poses only. Independent reference/GT is scoring-only. No reference-mesh normals, oracle-selected frames, GT pose initialization, or favorable per-scene threshold search. If a public example uses GT poses, label it mapping-only—not localization performance—and do not feed them to our primary pipeline.
- Select calibration/training sequences separately and lock parameters before held-out scenes. Report all runs, missing maps, failed tracking and abstentions. For active views, restrict decisions to information available at that prefix of the sequence.
- Score first-hit bias/MAE/P95/P99, missing-return rate, surface accuracy **and completeness**, near-field bins, incidence/confidence bins, floor residuals, low/foot/body/head hazard recall, drop misses, false blockage, and collision-free **complete** route success. Report per-scene/worst-scene tails; pooled metrics cannot hide a dangerous subgroup. Check continuous swept body volume, not waypoints alone.
- New critical false-free/drop/low-obstacle misses reject a variant even if mean mesh error improves. No degraded tail is silently traded against average gains. Also require useful route completion: refusing every path is not 100% accuracy. Predeclare acceptable noninferiority tolerances and confidence intervals; paired scene/route comparisons, not correlated frames treated as independent trials.
- Record wall time, peak memory, per-frame p95 latency, and later phone thermal/battery behavior. Static replay cannot establish cue latency or live reaction safety. Controlled adult device verification and separate child protocol remain necessary.

## 1. Sensor-specific range/angle/confidence weights, not blanket smoothing

**Failure addressed / hypothesis.** Distant/grazing, edge-mixed or low-confidence returns contribute disproportionate smear and ripple. Weighting trustworthy observations more strongly may improve geometry without filling unseen space. Highest near-term value, CPU-feasible in an existing integrator.

**Primary evidence and exact input.** [Nguyen–Izadi–Lovell, 3DIMPVT 2012](https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~nguyen/papers/conferences/Nguyen2012-ModelingKinectSensorNoise.pdf) measures axial/lateral Kinect noise against distance and incidence and applies it to fusion/tracking. Its coefficients are **Kinect-specific**, not iPhone calibration. Original standalone released implementation/license was not established in this pass; do not claim a drop-in package. [Apple's ARKit 4 explanation](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10611/) supplies the practical phone inputs: depth, confidence, frame pose and intrinsics; depth is distance from camera plane, confidence has low/medium/high categories, and depth resolution differs from RGB. The categories are not calibrated probabilities. Apple [sample source](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/arkit/displaying-a-point-cloud-using-scene-depth) is available; its upsampling is visualization, not extra observed geometry. Sample license/version must be reviewed before source reuse; platform API use remains subject to Apple SDK terms.

**Minimal fixed-data experiment.** Keep poses/mapper unchanged; compare baseline weights, confidence-only, then pre-calibrated range+angle+confidence weights. Derive normals from local valid depth neighborhoods without crossing discontinuities. Retain uncertainty/unknown where measurements are rejected. Calibrate a small lookup table on disjoint stationary planar/edge recordings, then freeze it; no copied Kinect constants. Keep unsmoothed and smoothed ARKit depth as separately named inputs if both were recorded—never conflate them.

**Gate / blocker / real data.** Reject if thin/low-object recall or missing-return coverage worsens despite cleaner planes. Repeated correlated frames must not collapse variance to zero. Public existing phone replay can compare confidence weighting only if confidence was saved; it cannot reconstruct absent confidence or calibrate sensor bias from an unverified floor. No new hardware beyond LiDAR iPhone for final calibration; desktop CPU replay is plausible, not measured. No verified public stairs-specific success for this technique was found.

## 2. Normal-aware non-projective TSDF: Voxfield ablation, not stock Voxblox repeat

**Failure addressed / hypothesis.** Projective ray distance overestimates normal surface distance at oblique views; correcting it may improve floor/wall distances and reduce voxel-scale bias. This does not fix pose drift, occlusion or missing floor.

**Released source.** [Official Voxfield](https://github.com/v4rl-ucy/voxfield), IROS 2022, BSD-3-Clause, inspected `main` (immutable SHA/tag not verified; **not adoption-ready**). CPU C++ core, Eigen/glog/threading; documented wrapper is ROS/catkin with Voxblox-family dependencies. Actual source [header](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/v4rl-ucy/voxfield/main/voxblox/include/voxblox/integrator/np_tsdf_integrator.h) accepts `T_G_C`, camera-frame points, normals and colors. [Implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/v4rl-ucy/voxfield/main/voxblox/src/integrator/np_tsdf_integrator.cc) uses measured normals/accumulated gradients for correction and skips extreme grazing contributions. Header default `normal_available=false` means simply running the binary is not proof normal-aware correction ran. Range validity uses point norm; over-range returns can become clearing rays when clearing is enabled. Normal arrays must match points, remain finite and be transformed as directions.

**Minimal fixed-data experiment.** Use a separate dependency/build directory (future authorization required): Voxfield intentionally names its package `voxblox`, so never replace the current benchmark build. The released `np_tsdf_server` differs from `tsdf_server`. Compare projective/non-projective paths with identical points, poses, resolution, truncation, range/clearing policy, threading and normals provenance. Run one locked parameter set, no scene-by-scene tuning. Score near-field and incidence bins and first-hit tails, not just meshes. CPU/iPhone transfer candidate is the C++ core through existing adapter; iOS compilation/performance unverified.

**Gate / public evidence.** Reject greater false-free volume, vanished thin objects, nonfinite normals/distances, or loss of coverage from grazing rejection. Cow & Lady real RGB-D and KITTI real LiDAR examples are documented; MaiCity is synthetic. These are not proof of phone stair/drop navigation. Paper PDF linked by the repo was blocked during this pass; source behavior above was directly inspected. Do not import a thesis percentage as a promised project gain.

## 3. Joint pose/calibration refinement with held-out residual validation

**Failure addressed / hypothesis.** Wrong intrinsics/depth deformation and small frame-pose errors can produce floor ripples even with a good fusion rule. Fixing calibration before integration may outperform smoothing. Prioritize diagnosis now; do not duplicate the running Open3D registration A/B.

**Primary/released evidence.** [BAD SLAM, CVPR 2019](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/papers/Schops_BAD_SLAM_Bundle_Adjusted_Direct_RGB-D_SLAM_CVPR_2019_paper.pdf) jointly optimizes poses, surfels and intrinsics using geometric/photometric evidence. [Official code](https://github.com/ETH3D/badslam), BSD (third-party licenses separate), has [release v1.1.0, short commit 25e7b8d](https://github.com/ETH3D/badslam/releases). Version is discovery evidence; full immutable revision and license tree remain to verify. Inputs: associated RGB/depth, pinhole intrinsics, units, valid capture timing. Linux/Windows NVIDIA CUDA compute capability >=5.3; tested GPU examples GTX 1070/1080. Dependencies include CUDA, Eigen, g2o, SuiteSparse, Qt and older OpenCV; **not an iPhone/CPU drop-in**.

**Critical compatibility.** [Camera documentation](https://github.com/ETH3D/badslam/blob/master/applications/badslam/doc/camera.md) assumes synchronized global-shutter RGB and depth; self-calibration can reduce robustness and should be a separate calibration step, then fixed. Its stereo-derived depth-noise model is not an iPhone LiDAR model. Therefore use as a controlled offline diagnostic only if assumptions are met, not presumed replacement for ARKit.

**Minimal experiment / gate.** After existing pose A/B result, freeze fusion and compare original poses, accepted estimated pose refinement, and independently calibrated-then-fixed intrinsics. Keep calibration and held-out mapping sequences separate. Inspect depth/RGB reprojection residuals across image/range, loop drift and map tails; do not choose alignment against scoring GT. Reject worse held-out error, implausible calibration, tracking loss, or hazard recall despite lower optimization loss. Public [ETH3D](https://www.eth3d.net/slam_overview) provides real RGB-D motion-capture benchmarking; it is not phone/stair-route evidence. Data license must be checked per downloaded subset; synthetic TUM-derived data is distinct.

## 4. Uncertainty-aware ground support plus independent obstacle envelope

**Failure addressed / hypothesis.** Floor facets block good routes, but smoothing can erase toys or bridge drops. Model support confidence explicitly while keeping obstacles in full 3-D. This is a narrow adapter on upstream mapping, not permission to invent a replacement mapper.

**Reference and inputs.** [Probabilistic terrain mapping / released elevation_mapping](https://github.com/ANYbotics/elevation_mapping), RA-L 2018, BSD-3-Clause, inspected `master`, no verified pin; repository explicitly no longer actively maintained. It accepts pose uncertainty and range measurements for robot-centric height estimation. [Actual fusion source](https://github.com/ANYbotics/elevation_mapping/blob/master/elevation_mapping/src/ElevationMap.cpp) weights elevation by variance, maintains bounds/time and uses visibility checks for cleanup. This supports a comparator, not wholesale adoption of a 2.5-D robot terrain map. CPU C++/ROS/grid_map dependency work is required; phone port and covariance availability unverified.

**Minimal experiment.** Same pose/depth stream; add measured floor-height interval, observation freshness/view provenance and support count alongside unchanged 3-D occupied/unknown evidence. Treat multiple heights, incompatible normals, absence of returns and occluded floor as ambiguity, not automatically ground. Compare facet-only versus support-confidence gating; keep low obstacle points independent from the floor fit, even if classed as outliers. Hold clearance/route planner fixed.

**Gate / data.** Fail any toy/threshold erased, drop bridged, or overhang collapsed into ground. Score floor false blockage separately from low-obstacle/drop recall and useful full-route completion. Existing Apple/MuSHRoom sequences only cover what was recorded; missing stair labels mean no stair pass. The method cannot infer support behind the sofa. Real stairs/overhang recordings with independently surveyed support labels remain needed. No cost saving from collapsing the whole world into one height value is acceptable.

## 5. Coverage-driven reacquisition instead of hallucinated completion

**Failure addressed / hypothesis.** Hidden furniture backs and unseen support are information deficits, not smoothing problems. Request a stationary view that reduces uncertainty specifically in the prospective corridor. More frames from the same direction do not guarantee independent evidence.

**Released reference.** [Receding-horizon next-best-view planner](https://github.com/ethz-asl/nbvplanner), ICRA 2016, `master`; package version 0.0.0, no verified immutable pin. [Source header](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ethz-asl/nbvplanner/master/nbvplanner/include/nbvplanner/nbvp.h) explicitly Apache-2.0. Inputs include point clouds, pose/covariance and known/unknown occupancy. Reference stack: C++ CPU, ROS Indigo/Jade-era, OctoMap, Eigen/catkin. Transfer information-gain scoring and visibility checks, **not autonomous drone motion commands**. No verified phone/child stair-reacquisition evidence; see robotics report for companion architecture detail.

**Minimal fixed-recording experiment.** Reveal only a time prefix. Rank reachable, stationary camera-look directions by reduction of uncertainty in the intended corridor; choose only among views actually recorded later. Compare against the same count/budget of chronological or uniform frames. Never consult withheld geometry to choose the best view. If required viewpoint was never recorded, report unobservable rather than synthesize it. Real re-aiming behavior requires a later authorized device trial.

**Gate / compute.** Track unsafe route acceptance before/after, unresolved support, low-object recall, extra frames/time and unnecessary prompts. Fail if the policy asks movement into unverified space, counts interpolation as observation, or gains coverage by accepting uncertainty. CPU local ray queries are plausible; branch/ROS dependency burden makes algorithm-reference reuse preferable. Approval of a map or repeat view cannot establish fresh clearance by itself.

## 6. Temporal foreground/background separation, with decay to unknown

**Failure addressed / hypothesis.** Moving furniture/people leave ghost obstacles or stale safe routes. Separate transient evidence from persistent background and require new observed free rays to clear previously occupied regions. Never delete a toy because it belongs to a dynamic object class.

**Released source/evidence.** [Dynablox](https://github.com/ethz-asl/dynablox), RA-L 2023, BSD-3-Clause; `main`, no verified immutable SHA, tag page showed no releases. Source and scripts are available; reference environment Ubuntu 20.04/ROS Noetic/C++ volumetric mapping, not a pretrained semantic model. Inputs: timestamped point clouds, estimated poses/transforms and historical map/free-space evidence. [NVIDIA's documented adaptation](https://nvidia-isaac-ros.github.io/v/release-3.1/concepts/scene_reconstruction/nvblox/technical_details.html), release-3.1 documentation, explains high-confidence free-space intrusion detection and decay toward unknown; accurate pose is required and motion slower than odometry drift may not be detected. This is not a recommendation to install CUDA nvblox on iPhone.

**Minimal experiment / gate.** Freeze poses and replay actual object/person crossings; compare static fusion with temporally separated occupancy. Score time-to-detect, stale-occupied removal, false clearing, static-object retention, and current swept-route safety. Include a person who stops, a moved chair and temporarily occluded small object. Decay may invalidate knowledge; it cannot prove free. Fail any dynamic-mask deletion without transient hazard retention, or any clearing based only on elapsed time. CPU core adaptation may be feasible; latency/memory/phone port unverified.

**Real stairs data.** [Official Dynablox dataset](https://projects.asl.ethz.ch/datasets/dynablox/) explicitly includes `stairs`, `ramp_1`, `ramp_2`, atypical moving objects and eight base sequences. Sensor is a **128-beam OS0 LiDAR**, not iPhone depth. Raw data includes IMU/VI estimate; processed data uses FAST-LIO2 estimates/undistortion and typically omits the first second. Treat versions separately. The [data DOI](https://doi.org/10.3929/ethz-c-000788607) was inaccessible through the browser, so data license/download size and exact annotations remain unverified—no download authorized/performed. This is valuable dynamic/stairs stress evidence, not ground-truth proof of safe foot support.

## Mandatory downstream control: exact clearance without false precision

Reuse the already-successful exact-EDT diagnostic and the companion robotics review's DynamicEDT3D audit rather than start another planner comparison. [SciPy 1.15.3 distance_transform_edt](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.15.3/reference/generated/scipy.ndimage.distance_transform_edt.html) defines exact Euclidean distance to background sample points and supports physical sampling. **Exact to grid centers is not exact to real surfaces.** Conservative occupied-cell extents, discretization error, localization/sensor uncertainty and continuous swept-body checks still matter. Unknown support/body corridor must not become free through the EDT. For each of the six experiments keep this oracle and margins unchanged; grid rotations/phase shifts and thin obstacles expose discretization luck. Any positive margin intrusion remains a hard failure. See companion report for licenses/native deployment candidate; no additional EDT execution happened here.

## Ready-to-authorize experiment order and stopping rules

1. Finish existing same-data registration A/B and retain near-field/tail breakdown; do not launch duplicate mapper builds.
2. Audit sensor contract and availability of saved confidence/normals/timing. Missing evidence blocks the dependent test, not the entire research program.
3. Locked confidence/range weighting ablation on current data; then isolated Voxfield projective/non-projective comparison, only after explicit resource authorization, immutable source/dependency pins and local adapter inspection.
4. Support-confidence + unchanged obstacle envelope on identical data; paired coverage-prefix experiment where views exist.
5. Dynamic real-data evaluation only after dataset licensing/size and pose-input provenance are verified. Later, controlled phone recordings establish platform transfer.

Accept only a reproducible improvement on the predeclared metric vector, with no new critical failures and useful completed routes. Stop/reject a candidate on failed hard gates; diagnose before tuning. If no candidate passes, report that negative result. No paper average, visually clean mesh, static route, star count or released source proves continuous blind-navigation safety.
