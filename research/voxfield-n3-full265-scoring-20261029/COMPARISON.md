# N3 normal-provider comparison: unchanged real-data geometry score

**Measured outcome: mixed, not an across-the-board improvement.** N3 gains some reference-paired coverage and slightly improves median/±5 cm agreement versus C1_N2, but pooled p95/p99/mean and longer-reference-range tails worsen. Exact-common-paired analysis confirms that the pooled/far-tail regression is not merely a change of hit subset. Do not claim an overall winner or promote a synthetic normal improvement into a navigation claim.

## Experiment, preserved failures, and validation

- Same original 265 training frames / 164,771,607 admitted points; same D1 core, original pixel inputs/order/K/poses/colors, TSDF/NP/mesh parameters. C1_N3 changes only the tested numerical-normal provider: four-export-pixel support plus intermediate-validity policy, rather than N2 one-pixel support, for this upsampled Koivu export. This is not a universal native-phone stencil or pure angle-only ablation.
- First N3 full attempt remains ENVIRONMENT_INTERRUPTED at 159 completed / 160 attempted frames, no final mesh/resource receipt, cap compliance unknown. The separately admitted retry starts from an empty map; no TSDF resume. The earlier N2 three-arm 300-CPU-second failure and its separate B/C0/C1 recovery are also preserved.
- Same 29 heldout views, original poses/K, 1,335,885 integer-pixel stride-four rays, immutable FARO cache, original 2/5/10 cm bands and depth strata. No GT mapping input, fit, scale adjustment, excluded ray, classifier or mesh repair. Primary evaluator/summary differ only by paths and the existing tsdf-only guard.
- Primary score and pre-frozen secondary comparisons completed once inside the same 60 CPU / 90 wall-second / 1 CPU / 1 GiB RSS / 1 GiB output guard. Prior N2 118-pin output freeze and original source/input/cache pins verified unchanged before and after.
