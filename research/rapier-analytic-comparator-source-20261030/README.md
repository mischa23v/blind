## Shape and authority distinctions

The .45 × .30 × 1.60 m body and .15 m horizontal margin are unchanged. KCC uses the **full nominal cuboid**. Its extra .002 m numerical offset is not the baseline VTK .002 m lower-query-band exclusion. Every row labels this difference; baseline VTK still excludes the foot-to-foot+.002 m slice. This is a labelled diagnostic shape difference, not parity or body shrinking.

Zero-input depenetration is a proposed correction only. Turns are unsupported without a KCC call; endpoint poses do not prove swept-turn clearance. `grounded` is a broad contact-distance/normal flag, not a 15-degree floor/support certificate. No source face is semantically filtered. Float32 exact aliases and vertex reindexing must be recorded, never interpreted as evidence that a hazard disappeared. Triangle ordinal/XYZ/winding are verified, but KCC collision events expose no original triangle ID; independent VTK witnesses remain separate.

Source-specific caveat: core 0.36.0 KCC uses translational casts; inspected Parry v0.31.1 cast code ignores normal constraints. Internal-edge correction applies to manifolds/grounding, not all cast normals. The two-sided flag reduces one-sided backface risk but is not a guarantee of complete hazard detection. No internal-edge improvement is promised.
