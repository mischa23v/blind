# Data license and attribution

Original data: Xuqian Ren / MuSHRoom, *iPhone dataset (Basic Data)*, published 2023-11-30, DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10230733 ; and *Reference Mesh and Point Cloud*, published 2023-11-29, DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10222321 . Both included official metadata snapshots declare `cc-by-4.0`: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Original archive hashes and the exact selected JPEG/mesh member identities remain in the receipts.

The eight JPEG exemplars are unmodified original processed-capture images. The eight NPZs are derived sampled depth/reference-comparison arrays; analytical PNGs are new displays of those saved arrays. Attribution and provenance are retained; the diagnostic transformations are expressly identified. No endorsement by the data authors is implied. All RGB exemplars were visually and metadata inspected before inclusion, without changing their bytes.

No additional application-source license is invented by this evidence packet. VTK, NumPy, Pillow and Matplotlib implementations/binaries are not redistributed here. The code invokes existing installed implementations; primary source/version references remain in the reviews and protocols.
