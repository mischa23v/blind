**Coordinate qualification:** all projections below are conditional on the documented frozen offline transforms, not independently verified physical pixel-to-target correspondences. The raw Polycam OBJ is unaligned with poses; the source planner uses a separate historical rigid fit, and this diagnostic projects saved original-model rays/FARO hits—not raw OBJ vertices. See `COORDINATE_PROVENANCE_REVIEW.md` for the exact formulas, source hashes and limits.

Three original public MuSHRoom Koivu RGB-D export pairs were inspected without changing the frozen matrix, maps, support labels or thresholds. No mesh was loaded, no new data downloaded and no imagery generated. This is exploratory post-outcome reference-versus-phone diagnosis, not an additional matrix result or Apple/device validation.

Selection used original poses/intrinsics before reading the chosen pixels, not a depth-error criterion. The third-view rule was declared after inspecting the first two frames, so this remains an explicitly exploratory inspection. Original and author-associated poses/intrinsics agree exactly for all selected frames. Existing stage84 transform and camera-ray functions were reused without VTK imports; fixed author registration was not fitted. Projection retains OpenGL camera-to-world, negative Z forward, per-frame pinhole calibration and half-pixel screen centres; no interpolation or hole filling.

**Units and missing evidence:** the archive contains published **processed Polycam UInt16 depth exports**, converted by the reviewed author factor0.001 to nominal axial metres—not raw ARKit sensor buffers or independently validated metric truth. Native raw depth/confidence, tracking and capture timestamps are unavailable; RGB-depth registration and phone-to-FARO calibration have no independently measured uncertainty bound here. Missing confidence was not replaced or invented. The cleaned/hole-completed FARO mesh lacks a measured-versus-edited face mask. Neither image appearance nor missing geometry establishes clearance, a drop, continuous support or safe guidance.
