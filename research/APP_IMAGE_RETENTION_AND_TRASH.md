## Proposed retention contract

1. **Temporary processing by default.** Ordinary perception consumes short-lived frames without a gallery write. Bound outstanding work, image dimensions, frame count, byte use and age; release cancelled/background/session-ended work as soon as native consumers finish. Cancelled callbacks cannot persist or restore deleted material. Privacy settings must distinguish camera awareness from permission to learn persistent room references.
2. **Keep useful evidence, not every repeated view.** Persist only explicitly user-kept images or selected keyframes with a declared local purpose and ownership. Prefer adequate quality, viewpoint/coverage diversity and measured novelty over oldest-entry rotation. When coverage is sufficient and no useful change is observed, stop adding/replacing redundant room references while allowing ephemeral perception. “Enough” means sufficient for the declared reference purpose, never complete room coverage, current clearance or proven localization. Preserve user-kept choices from automatic eviction; report capacity pressure rather than silently purge them.
3. **Bound retention end-to-end.** Specify and validate per-room, per-purpose and global count/byte limits, transient age limits, pending-work limits and bounded Trash capacity before implementation acceptance. Include full images, thumbnails, fingerprints, crop copies, labels/OCR, map databases, WAL/journals, temporary exports and caches. The current 16 room references is a useful existing bound, not a complete retention solution. Do not invent calibrated production limits in this tracker.
4. **Accessible review and Trash.** Provide voice and VoiceOver equivalents for Review saved images, Keep, Move to Trash, Restore, select/multiselect, room-scoped bulk removal and Empty Trash. Each entry exposes purpose, room, capture time if actually available, storage size and dependency impact; never fabricate a visual description. Identify exactly which item(s) a voice confirmation affects. Handle same-name rooms, focus changes, interruptions and stale confirmation without deleting the wrong record. No swipe-only controls. Announce actual completion/failure, counts and remaining cleanup, in supported Arabic/English.
5. **Trash is explicit retained data.** Explain that moving to Trash keeps a recoverable local copy and removes it immediately from active recognition/retrieval. Show retained bytes and the applicable retention policy. Restore only by explicit user action; do not automatically reactivate stale spatial authority or overwrite a newer revision. Permanent Delete/Empty Trash requires clear scoped confirmation. No silent permanent purge of user-kept or trashed images; if capacity is reached, stop new durable captures and offer review. A future automatic expiry requires an explicit user-approved policy. An immediate privacy-delete action must bypass Trash after its own clear confirmation.
6. **Respect dependency ownership.** Track stable reference IDs and exact room/map/frame/revision dependencies. Preserve independently usable layout/geometry when deleting an appearance image does not invalidate it. If a map/landmark needs the deleted raw image or its derived features, explain the affected recognition/localization capability, retire it and offer explicit rescan/rebuild; never retain a hidden image to keep the map usable. Deleting an image and its derivatives removes active thumbnails/crops/features/search indexes and invalidates queued uses. A separately offered geometry-only retention choice must disclose what stays and require an informed choice; do not disguise a fingerprint as anonymous harmless data.
7. **Crash-safe privacy semantics.** Reuse existing deletion tombstones, work revisions and storage owners. Persist revocation before asynchronously removing bytes; retries/restart cannot resurrect deleted data. If physical cleanup fails, keep it inaccessible, report “cleanup pending,” and retry safely—never claim full deletion. Document storage/OS limitations instead of promising forensic erasure. Broad room/place deletion remains authoritative over restoration or late learning. Prevent immediate automatic re-learning of a just-forgotten reference without an explicit, understandable retention choice.
8. **No new outbound path.** Review/Trash/restore, diagnostics and migration stay local. No images, sensitive labels, fingerprints or private maps enter GitHub/public exports/logs/telemetry/online backups. User-owned Photos assets and downloaded research datasets remain outside the app-owned cleanup scope.

## Acceptance evidence to collect later (not run)

- Repeated unchanged-room captures settle at the declared useful-reference limit, show no continued redundant disk growth, and continue required ephemeral perception. Changed views can add useful evidence only within caps and permissions. Measure memory/disk under long sessions and native-job cancellation.
- Individual, same-name-room, selected-set and room-wide review/Trash/restore work entirely by VoiceOver and voice in Arabic/English, including cancel, interruption, stale selection and partial storage failure.
- Trashed records cannot influence recognition, retrieval, map matching or spoken facts. Explicit restore reinstates only eligible retained records, not old pose/clearance. Empty Trash and immediate privacy deletion remove dependent copies/features or truthfully report pending cleanup.
- Keep a geometry-only map usable where independent; invalidate affected recognition/landmark/map revisions and request a rescan where deletion breaks dependencies. Test shared deduplicated storage without cross-room leakage or hidden retention.
- Race deletion against active learning, OCR, map save, delayed callback, restart, migration and restore. Verify tombstones prevent resurrection and broad deletion wins. Verify no automatic reacquisition silently undoes the user's privacy intent.
- Inventory on-device storage, including optional upstream mapping DB and serialized world maps; inspect backup exclusions, temporary files, WAL/journals, cache thumbnails and debug exports. Verify no network payload or system Photos deletion. These checks must use authorized local fixtures, not publish household images.

## Open design decisions

Numerical storage/retention budgets, sufficient-coverage/novelty criteria, migration of existing anonymous array entries to stable IDs, upstream mapping dependency granularity, and Trash expiry policy require implementation design and measured validation. Current source lacks per-reference capture-time metadata in RoomRecall.Stored; UI must say unavailable for legacy entries rather than infer it. This backlog does not lower independent live-hazard/localization release gates.
