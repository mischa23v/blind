# Fixed planar physics and seed-connected local ground membership

This is an explicitly controlled simulation model. It is not live sensed clearance, semantic ground truth, toy detection, or proof that support is continuous between the nine probes.

The previous v2 ground component is the training prior: original shared-edge-connected faces with absolute model-Y normals within 5 degrees, seeded by all nine original initial support hit IDs. Preserve this entire component, including sparse large floor triangles. Fit ONE broad plane to its unique original vertices with upstream SciPy `least_squares`, `soft_l1`, scale 0.002 m, at most 100 evaluations. Use all vertices up to 50,000; above this, deterministic evenly spaced original-ID order selects fitting vertices. Report residual quantiles over ALL training vertices, not only the fitting sample. Plane slope must remain at most 5 degrees.

The broad plane supplies the declared flat world support. It does not define a route-wide 2 mm membership band. To recover noisy microfacets excluded by v2, use fixed 0.20 m square patches centred on a 0.10 m lattice at model-XZ origin zero. Original triangle centroid selects the patch. Fit only ORIGINAL training-component vertices in that patch (never objects or newly recovered faces), at most 4,096 deterministic samples. A recovered original face needs finite local fit, fit slope at most 5 degrees, every original vertex at most 0.002 m from that local plane, raw facet slope at most 45 degrees, and the unchanged route-envelope bounds. Recompute original shared-edge connectivity and retain only the component containing all initial seeds. No iterative expansion or contact-specific refit.

The 2 mm local tolerance is a disclosed unresolved microgeometry band. Objects in that band are not proved detectable. The 1 cm toy, vertical riser, bump and drop fixtures remain non-ground; disconnected flat tops cannot become ground just because their height is small. A larger gentle feature connected to the training floor can remain part of this model: these rules are not semantic object classification.

Only local candidate vertices are transformed, never the full reference mesh. Existing two-million candidate cap, fixed route envelope plus 0.8 m and nominal Y range [-0.25,0.25] remain. Original reference faces and obstacle collision geometry are unchanged. Missing support, disconnected support and non-ground first-hit surfaces remain failures. The world runner must record raw support-ray values separately from its flat-plane actor Y and enforce its unchanged association/hole/contact rules. No reference result selects or changes the phone route.

The abandoned initial-nine-point/global-2-mm proposal is preserved as `initial-plane-proposal.not-executed.py`; it was never frozen or used in a recorded run. Its brittle global-band abstraction was rejected before execution.
