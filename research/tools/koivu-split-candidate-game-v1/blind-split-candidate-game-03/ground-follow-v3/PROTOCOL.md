# Same-route controlled planar-ground game diagnostic

This is the requested simplified flat-ground experiment. It changes the **simulation world's support model only**, not the provided phone map, route, controller, actor XZ/yaw, body dimensions, margin or physical authorization. The previous fixed-foot attempt (17.54 cm) and raw-microfacet ground attempt (31.54 cm) remain preserved failures, with no arrival. A tiny-initial-patch/global-2-mm-plane proposal was rejected before execution and preserved as an unexecuted source cut.

## Fixed ground/model boundary

1. In the same fixed route envelope plus 0.8 m horizontally and nominal Y [-0.25,+0.25] m, retain the original reference face IDs. At most two million candidates. No full-reference adjacency graph or transformed whole-mesh copy.
2. Use the unchanged initially seeded, original-shared-edge, <=5-degree face component as the broad ground **training assumption**. Require all nine original support faces. This is an explicitly selected simulation floor region, not independent semantic floor truth.
3. Fit ONE robust support plane to its unique original vertices, using NumPy least squares initialization and SciPy `least_squares(loss='soft_l1', f_scale=0.002)`. At most 50,000 deterministically original-ID-ordered samples for fitting; compute residual quantiles on every training vertex. Plane slope at most 5 degrees. The plane remains fixed for the whole episode; it is not refit to contacts or goal progress.
4. Retain established training ground. Recover excluded noisy microfaces only with fixed **20 cm square local fit patches on a 10 cm lattice**, trained exclusively on that original ground. Local fitting uses the same robust loss and 2 mm scale, at most 4,096 deterministic training points; local slope at most 5 degrees. Every original candidate vertex must lie within 2 mm of its assigned local plane, and its raw facet slope must be <=45 degrees. This is a local residual rule, **not a 2 mm route-wide flat-floor band**. Recovered faces must join the original shared-edge seeded component; no coordinate welding, hole bridging, iterative component hopping or contact-specific exception.
5. All other original geometry remains an obstacle collider. Detached 1 cm tops, vertical risers, drop boundaries, walls and head-height geometry are not deleted because they are low or approximately horizontal. The unresolved 2 mm microgeometry band is explicit; detection of every object inside it is not claimed. A shallow ramp-like object remains a semantic ambiguity.

## Actual physics and acceptance

The world replaces selected ground support with the ONE fitted plane. Its foot anchor is the maximum plane height at the nine original actual-body support positions. Before every half-step, the unchanged nine raw reference rays must still hit selected ground and satisfy the unchanged finite support spread/association checks. Thus an infinite mathematical plane does not fill missing mesh holes or permit a disconnected object top. Raw floor height variation no longer bounces the body or defines microfacet walkability.

Nominal body is 0.45 m width × 0.30 m depth × 1.60 m height, with a separate 0.15 m horizontal margin once. The 2 mm contact exclusion is unchanged. Exact original triangle geometry remains for non-ground swept collisions. Each proposed 15 Hz actor increment is split into two continuous enclosed sweeps, including rotation and plane-induced vertical motion. Total 3D translation <=2.5 cm; yaw <=1 degree. Nominal horizontal speed 0.30 m/s, maximum 0.375 m/s. No XZ snap, route correction, start replacement, or reference steering.

Same FIRST_ROUTE, baseline condition, 1,200-tick cap. First non-ground actual-body or margin contact terminates; each run event is counted once, not once per triangle. Ground triangle reports are separate and may repeat across envelopes/half-steps. Arrival requires delivered goal stop, actual XZ error <=0.35 m, zero applied/pending movement, and nonzero sufficient actual travel. Unknown support, setup failure, contact, deadlock and timeout are not arrivals. All permissive unresolved phone-map flags remain logged. No cane catches are assumed or credited.

Same budgets: 1,800 CPU seconds / 2,700 wall seconds / 3 GiB process-tree RSS / 64 MiB outputs / 768 MiB minimum free. Source and input pins are checked before and after. Focused fixtures and source review precede the recorded run. This single-room offline game cannot prove live iPhone obstacle safety, current coverage, stairs or general terrain handling.
