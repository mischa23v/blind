# Koivu floor/routing diagnostics — review checkpoint

**Question:** why does the supplied phone-derived routing mask block parts of the recorded camera passage, and can a better floor model address that without deleting low objects or inventing support?

This packet preserves completed diagnostics only. It contains no new movement outcome, no active two-approach executor, and no claim that a fitted plane makes a route safe. The numeric profiles are abstract geometry configurations, not participant measurements.

## Main findings

- **Camera/mask mismatch:** 39 of 294 camera centres lie in blocked mask cells. In the western region, 33 of 71 camera records are blocked (20 low-height flags, 13 missing-support flags); those 71 records occupy 57 unique cells. Frame 214 approaches the first camera position within 0.339645 m after approximately 1.003913 turns around the fixed proxy. Camera centre is not body centre; straight joining chords are not measured continuous motion, and the target is not a verified semantic table.
- **No gross adapter unit/scale/axis defect was found in the inspected calculations**, not proof of absolute physical dimensions. Map spacing is 0.05 m; level rotation is orthonormal; processed millimetre depth converts by 0.001. The reference similarity scale is approximately 1.020790844 and is not used by the phone route adapter. See `CAMERA_SCALE_PROVENANCE.md`: these calculations were ad-hoc heredocs whose executable scripts were **not retained**. Preserved JSON/plots and input identities are provided, not a false rerunnable historical script.
- **Profile and topology failures remain failures:** the fixed full-loop construction failed for a 45 cm raw width with zero margin; the same body with 5 cm margin per side (55 cm effective width); and an abstract 28×30 cm profile. Its separately preregistered 0.125 m proposal-radius fallback also failed. Zero movement episodes occurred. Point-mask topology can enclose the original target, while the 0.375 m centre mask has no bounded regions. The necessary 0.30 m inscribed-disc diagnostic has 2,942 centres across six components and no enclosure. These are finite-grid/model findings, not proof against every orientation-aware physical route.
- **Original processed-depth evidence:** 13,415,808 source samples across all 294 frames yielded 8,567 endpoints in the fixed 57 western columns. Of 15 unique low-flag blocked cells, 12 have near-range endpoints in the low-height band. Distinct views disagree: affected frames 62–64 place selected near-floor-looking pixels roughly 6–7 cm above the global plane. This is not merely absence of depth; native confidence/tracking are unavailable. All endpoints remain retained.
- **Saved reference join:** 25 unique western cells are blocked. Thirteen have prior full-triangle band tests with no contact from 1 cm through 1.8 m and five of five support-ray hits at approximately −4.71 to −2.72 mm. The other 12 have vertex-only evidence and must **not** be called clear. The join checked identical R/t/grid origin and reused saved reference arrays; it did not query a new reference or produce perfect ground truth.
- **Signed-key indexing is a real but tiny contributor here:** correcting only signed-Y-aware X decoding changes 796 of 1,384 fit rows. The reconstructed original transform matches the supplied transform to about 9.2e−16. Maximum nominal plane-height change is 0.263 mm over the fixed map and **0.0204 mm** in the western cells—far too small to explain the centimetre-scale discrepancy. No fusion, mask or route was changed.
- **Established per-view fit-only check:** Open3D 0.19.0 `segment_plane` produced 236 valid and 58 invalid frame fits under the frozen estimation gates. In affected frames 62–64, western endpoint median residuals are approximately 0.75, 1.15 and 1.44 mm relative to their fitted planes, versus global-plane median heights around 61–66 mm. This supports investigating view-dependent floor/pose consistency, **not clearing those points**. The 1 cm estimation tolerance is not an object-clearance rule. Original points, residuals, invalid fits and unknown support remain; no mapper adopted the fits.

## Questions for external review

1. Does one global floor/height band misclassify this passage across views? What established local/per-view floor representation and pose-consistency test should replace or supplement it?
2. How should evidence distinguish sloping/noisy floor from a 1 cm object, a shallow ramp, a step edge or an unseen hole—without erasing inliers or extrapolating plane support into missing regions?
3. How should the camera-path contradiction be interpreted given camera/body offsets, discrete poses and processed depth? What independent measurements are still needed before changing walkability?
4. Are the raw endpoint-to-frame/cell associations and exact saved-reference join correct? Which conclusions are justified for the 13 triangle-tested cells versus the 12 vertex-only cells?
5. Given the small signed-key-only effect and lack of a gross unit defect, what quantitative cross-view/pose experiment would discriminate calibration, drift, surface variation and global-fit bias?
6. Are the native discretization, proposal discs and fixed compass construction too restrictive relative to the preserved rectangle/turn checks? A weaker proposal filter must still pass those checks; a camera trace is not an authorized body route.
