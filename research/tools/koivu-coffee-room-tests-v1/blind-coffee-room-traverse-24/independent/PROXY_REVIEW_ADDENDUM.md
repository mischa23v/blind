**The source/claim boundary is appropriate, subject to the qualifications below.** This addendum does not replace the earlier route review. No planner query, NPZ materialization or map edit was performed by this reviewer.

Result hashes match the phone-only localization and original route manifest.
- Independent JSON recount reproduced six blocked subsets and all their diagnostic counts. `RESULT.json` and `COUNTS.json` agree. The 32 saved critical witness cells generate exactly 436 neighborhood cells by ±3 source-cell offsets. There are 158 unique blocked-row records within that neighborhood.
- The blocked rows partition in this actual saved result as **93 low/body-geometry proxies, 47 floor-present/no-saved-obstacle proxies and 18 no-floor proxies**. The high-obstacle subset partitions as **92 / 10 / 18**, total 120. These three groups happen to be disjoint and exhaustive for these rows; other diagnostic categories and phone flags overlap and must not be summed blindly.
- The older checkpoint is explicitly `b0be8628b159e0fc0446a7af8c57c13c99553a77`, not the latest Opus color/v3 pipeline. This review neither tested nor established availability/performance of that newer candidate.

## Necessary interpretation limits

1. **One witness neighborhood, not all room barriers.** The localization selects one endpoint-0-to-2 point-grid witness and its 32 minimum-clearance cells. The 436-cell neighborhood and 158 blockers are not an exhaustive enumeration of every possible bottleneck, blocking surface or room cell. The 1.464 cm reported threshold is a grid-centre EDT lower-bound diagnostic, not measured physical passage width. No physical impossibility follows.
2. **The floor proxy is permissive.** The upstream source computes `ref_floor_h` as the average of reference vertices in the interval (-0.06, +0.06) m relative to the fitted plane. Floor-proxy presence does not refute a near-dip flag or missing stricter phone support. Thus the 47 proxy-only columns—particularly the 15 near-dip columns—are follow-up locations, not established false phone blockers or proven clear floor.
3. **Reference geometry is band-limited vertex evidence.** Upstream low-band maxima use [0.015, 0.30) m, and this diagnostic tests maxima >=0.025 m; body-band maxima use [0.30, 1.80) m. That is not an exact collision test for the 1.60 m body. Vertex absence is not triangle-surface absence, and vertex presence in a column is not a swept-body collision. All 47 blocked floor-only proxy rows happen to have no finite saved low-band maximum at all, but the general predicate only excludes low maxima >=2.5 cm plus body-band maxima.
4. **Even the point witness is not independently clear.** The saved summary has reference body-band vertices in 5 of the 32 minimum-clearance witness cells, and geometry proxies in 8 of 278 neighborhood walk cells. The point path must remain labeled a topological witness, never an alternate safe path.
5. **No source-frame or semantic cause established.** The diagnostic has collapsed flags/proxies, not raw Coffee images or occupied/free leaf clouds. It cannot identify which frame, object, doorway or sensor failure caused a blocker. No independent registration, triangle replay, live tracking or movement episode occurred. This is postrun explanatory triage, not a fresh held-out validation.

`PROXY_RECOUNT.json` pins the reviewed diagnostic artifacts and records the independently reproduced saved-row arithmetic; `recount_proxy_saved.py` reproduces it without importing planner code or opening NPZ arrays. The prior route manifest and review evidence remain unchanged.
