# N3 native-spacing normal provider — source preparation, no execution

## Hypothesis, not gain claim
The admitted first-three diagnostic established exported adjacent depth changes only at coordinate phase0 mod4, with63.2805% valid N2 normals exactly+opticalZ. N3 changes only normal support from +1 to +4 export pixels. This can reduce repeated-block derivative artifacts; it might also bridge short features, enlarge unavailable borders, and hurt reconstruction. No original full265 results are replaced and no full reconstruction is proposed here.

## Precise candidate
`n3_adapter.h` is an explicitly custom experimental adaptation, NOT a released normal estimator. Reuses unchanged N2 Raster/Result/unproject/tripletNormal and reason semantics. For every unchanged admitted0<mm<4000 center, endpoints(u+4,v),(u,v+4) replace +1 tangents. Every intermediate axial sample at offsets1,2,3,4 must also be admitted and differ by no more than5% of center depth (same integer diff*20>center rule). Missing precedence over edge, then finite double cross/norm>0, finite float unit output, same sign. Four right/bottom rows/columns unavailable; all admitted center points retained byte-for-byte and in original order, regardless of normal availability. No normal fill or inferred confidence.

This is a disclosed compound support adaptation: endpoint spacing and intermediate validity checks, not pure endpoint-spacing-only ablation. Intermediate checks prevent knowingly crossing missing/>5% discontinuous axial samples; they do NOT guarantee connected support throughout the triangle or across diagonal holes. Sub5% low objects may still yield spanning normals; those are reported, not classified away. No areaepsilon, physicalnoise threshold, floor/toy classifier, decimation, K/pose change, recenter or TSDF change.

## Phase/crop and native-center assumptions
Observed changes at start-coordinate0mod4 imply block starts at1mod4 in these exported rasters. Four-pixel endpoints preserve the same within-block phase across adjacent observed blocks at any center. N3 does not snap to a phase or reconstruct original native sample centers: exporter crop offsets/rotation/resampling revision and the actual native-center projection are unknown. It uses original perframeK/integer exported coordinates, keeping geometry fixed. Calling this native-spacing describes observed exported repetition pitch only, not a recovered raw LiDAR calibration. Generalization beyond these frames requires checking export provenance/pattern first. Block-start phases0,1,3 (transition phases3,0,2) synthetic controls are predeclared, not used to select an algorithm phase.

## Separate released comparator consideration (not executed or bundled)
Open3D0.19.0 PointCloud::EstimateNormals computes neighborhood covariance using KDTreeSearchParam, then an eigenvector normal. Official source: https://github.com/isl-org/Open3D/blob/v0.19.0/cpp/open3d/geometry/EstimateNormals.cpp and tutorial https://www.open3d.org/docs/release/tutorial/geometry/pointcloud.html . This is a plausible separately frozen released comparator, not automatically safer: nearest neighbors on repeated dense export samples may still remain inside one replicated block; larger radius may cross toys/drop/overhang surfaces. Neighbor support, degeneracy, normal sign and missing-data policy need a separate reviewed proposal, not silently default30neighbors or tutorialradius. No Open3D call or new package in current stage. Existing Open3D library has already been used elsewhere but its exact binary/closure would need repinning for any comparator.

Synthetic angle reporting renormalizes output float normals only for diagnostics; this does not modify provider normals. Its tiny ideal-angle floor is not numerically comparable to the previous unnormalized-dot diagnostic. Report common-valid4pixel-interior angle support alongside all-valid means.

Released Open3D0.19.0 EstimateNormals.cpp also substitutes(0,0,1) when a computed normal has zero norm and no prior normal exists (source lines in PointCloud::EstimateNormals). A drop-in call therefore cannot be treated as observed/confident normal availability without separate degeneracy/support accounting. This reinforces keeping that comparator separate from N3.
