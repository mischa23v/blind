Read RESULT.md and INDEPENDENT_COMPLETED_REVIEW.json. This is the completed provider-only experiment, not C1_N3 mapping, a full-room result or a new reconstruction score. The same 1,296,420 admitted points/order/coordinates are retained. There are **50,445 fewer numerically valid normals**: unavailable counts increase from 20,783 to 71,228. The much lower exact optical +Z fraction is a distribution change, not a real-normal error rate or confidence measurement.

Synthetic angular comparisons improve for the declared common-support examples, but actual recorded normal truth is unavailable. The close-range residual remains large. All predeclared phase and ideal/quantized controls are retained, not selected by best score. Angle reporting renormalizes float normals, unlike the earlier diagnostic's tiny raw-float angular floor. Do not equate these diagnostic changes with mapping gain.

N3 still permits bridging two/four-centimetre steps at two metres under the inherited depth gate. A diagonal interior hole outside its checked axial paths does not invalidate a normal. Four-row/column unavailable borders grow and native sample centres/exporter remain unknown. Inherited equal_depth_triplets and interior_positions log fields retain their offset-one meanings, not N3 support/eligible counts. These limitations are preserved, not silently fixed or relabelled. No mapper, mesh, GT, heldout scorer or physical-navigation evaluation ran here. Earlier mixed mapping results remain unchanged.
