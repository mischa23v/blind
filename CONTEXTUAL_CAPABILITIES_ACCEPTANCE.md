# Context-driven foreground capabilities

Source increment after primary `2377a0fee9a799394e5696f59d1bda081ae72d5c`, 2026-10-23. Product requirements are consolidated in [CONTINUOUS_COMPANION_DESIGN.md](CONTINUOUS_COMPANION_DESIGN.md). This is a bounded wiring increment, not completion of that vision or approval for unsupervised child use.

## Implemented connections

- The existing AR frame callback runs local hazard processing first, then admits one bounded nonurgent contextual job. Typed retained user goals choose relevant OCR, saved-product barcode, camera-light and isolated-item colour probes without requiring a feature tab or mode command. Ordinary conversation remains independent. Unknown goals do not become invented tasks; this is a bounded lexical adapter, not general semantic planning.
- A silent Vision rectangle probe can run without a money command when the existing Saudi reference backend/profile is available. The full uncropped capture goes to the existing reference verifier, never to a denomination-generating LLM. A rectangle is not proof of a banknote. Two separate fresh verified matching denominations are required for a passive amount; that temporal stability is not an independent accuracy estimate and correlated errors remain possible. Rejected proposals stay silent outside a payment goal; within that goal they may report only that no value was verified, not guessed handling directions.
- Probe success has a six-second base currency interval; an empty probe polls at twelve seconds. Actual failures back off, at most 120 seconds; missing backends do not run. Other work rotates with bounded intervals. These prototype intervals are not a measured instant-recognition guarantee. Expensive reference matching does not run on every frame.
- OCR selects a short high-confidence line relevant to the retained task; finding a book is not permission to read every sign. Money values, spatial commands and hazard text do not become unsolicited OCR guidance. Explicit whole-page reading retains its separate labelled-snapshot flow. Barcode discovery speaks only a locally saved product label, never an unsolicited raw code/URL, beep, unsupported global product lookup or proof of contents.
- Camera illumination is not a light-switch sensor or outdoor time-of-day. Colour uses a single isolated-instance mask and rejects overlapping people; see PRE_DEVICE_HARDENING.md for the stricter target limitations. It does not identify an arbitrary outfit, verify white versus cream/gray, or identify someone by clothing. Native uncertainty remains withheld rather than guessed.
- All ordinary candidates use existing signs/objects attention preferences, quiet state and word budget. Duplicate content is bounded; fresh capture, exact request/perception/session/goal lease and camera pose are checked again before delivery. Movement, hazards, interruption and lifecycle changes retire old output. Apple synchronous Vision cannot be forcibly terminated; abandoned work is tracked separately, and a timed-out producer is disabled for the process rather than stacking replacements.
- Familiar recognition restores only a non-biometric local on/off preference. Unset means eligible, not identified: a valid consented encrypted gallery, compatible pinned backend and existing capture/release gates remain required. An explicit saved off choice persists. Gallery loading no longer requires visiting People. First foreground unlock can retry unavailable protected storage without speaking or interrupting unrelated conversation.
- Enrollment keeps the phone with its wearer and asks the sighted participant to face the child/change angles. Saved examples require the existing simple purpose/consent and named-save flow; photo count is never consent. Passive recognition still has existing stationary/upright/tracked-capture requirements. It does not identify people from their back, clothing, a remembered position or a generic face description.
- Existing automatic saved-room recovery is reused unchanged. A room label/appearance remains a candidate, not localization or clearance.
- Direct voice/typed room-scan requests now use an explicit, expiring two-turn confirmation. Quoted, negative, conditional, model-tool and observed-text inputs cannot start it through this policy. The completed warning is bound to session/goal/perception state; interruption or lifecycle changes cancel it. Speech recognition cannot establish addressee identity. Stop finishes/saves, cancel discards. Continuous conversation is not deliberately ended by scan start; RoomPlan still interrupts phone-camera path monitoring, and the app says so. Independent wearable capability is not a promise of complete hazard coverage.

## Availability and outstanding limits

The ordinary Release build still has no approved Saudi production profile. The existing pinned Debug device-test configuration is required for reference recognition; this increment does not ship weights or references, change thresholds, or assert money accuracy. No general Detector model has been bundled. RGB camera startup is now independent of scene-depth availability; depth-dependent guidance remains unavailable without validated depth. See PRE_DEVICE_HARDENING.md.

Native iOS/Apple SDK compilation, model compatibility, accuracy, real VoiceOver/audible behavior, camera mounting, interruption timing, latency, battery/thermal load and actual household journeys remain pending. The system does not guarantee that everything enters the camera's field of view. Auto eligibility is not all-day operation, safe physical guidance or complete autonomous capability coverage. Current unvalidated physical direction/reach/pickup remains withheld.

## Mac/iPhone acceptance sequence

1. Build the exact chosen configuration, including preserved familiar-backend options and the separately approved Saudi Debug reference pack. Default/Release unavailability is expected, not a reason to disable evidence gates.
2. Open the eligible app without visiting feature tabs. Present known/unknown/noncurrency rectangles while seated; measure discovery and amount latency, rejection rate, false amounts and repeat burden. Test multiple/clipped/look-alike notes and both sides. Two-frame agreement alone is not the pass criterion.
3. Set a general shopping/reading/dressing goal. Verify useful task-related discoveries, no irrelevant label stream, no unsolicited beeps, signs/objects mute respected, unknown saved-product barcode silent, and no arbitrary clothing assignment.
4. With a consented gallery and compatible backend, verify first-session automatic hints, persisted off, once-per-encounter behavior, revocation/deletion and protected-storage unlock. Compare look-alikes; do not interpret a hint as pickup authorization.
5. Ask to scan a room, interrupt the warning, say yes after expiry, change goal/session, quote a scan instruction and discuss scanning with another person. No scan should start without the completed current confirmation. Verify stop/save versus cancel/discard, voice availability during scan, truthful camera-alert interruption and monitored restoration.
6. Interrupt every pending job with ordinary conversation, a simulated hazard, camera off, mute/end, background and goal change. Confirm no stale speech or stuck scheduler. Measure ordinary response, hazard onset, thermal/battery and working-set load with actual on-device models before supervised trials.

## Reused foundations

Apple [Vision rectangles](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/vision/vndetectrectanglesrequest), [text recognition](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/vision/vnrecognizetextrequest), [barcode detection](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/vision/vndetectbarcodesrequest), ARKit light estimation and RoomPlan are existing platform adapters. Existing Saudi reference matching, encrypted familiar gallery and colour masking retain their recorded model/source/data license and validation requirements. This increment adds domain scheduling/confirmation glue and tests, not a replacement recognizer, SLAM system, database, trained model or inference runtime.
