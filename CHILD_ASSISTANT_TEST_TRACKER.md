# Child assistant scenario and evidence tracker

Updated 2026-10-23. This is a bounded acceptance checklist for the integrated source, not proof of child safety, actual model accuracy, native accessibility, or measured responsiveness. No actual child, Apple model, iPhone, VoiceOver or physical hazard trial was executed for this checklist. **100% accuracy is not established.** Use simulated hazards and harmless stand-ins for dangerous objects; do not ask a child to discover a failure.

The product is a general free on-device companion for conversation, memory and everyday tasks, with navigation as one separately gated tool. Use this current matrix alongside [everyday companion acceptance](EVERYDAY_COMPANION_ACCEPTANCE.md), [spatial companion acceptance](SPATIAL_COMPANION_ACCEPTANCE.md) and the [feature matrix](ASSISTANT_FEATURE_CHECKLIST.md). Older checkpoint counts in those documents are historical, not current device acceptance.

The intended flow is: after adult setup and OS permissions, opening the app starts an idle voice conversation and permitted foreground sensing without a child pressing Start. Explicit mute/end and camera-off choices must survive background/foreground transitions. Background voice is opt-in; background camera remains off. The session policy is ephemeral: foreground-resume checks do not establish persistence of mute/end through process termination or reinstall. Permission denial must be reported truthfully, never described as listening or sensing.

The first tab shows the settled assistant reply only. Settings retains accessible conversation controls. Sensor hazards have an independent, code-owned priority path; this must not depend on an LLM finishing. A source call order is **not** proof of real-time scheduling or audible latency.

## Evidence rules used in every scenario

- **None:** ordinary conversation, arithmetic, word meanings and fictional discussion do not require camera, map, gallery or a sensor read. Avoid unnecessary perception/tool delay.
- **Device clock:** use a fresh canonical local clock record and the actual device time zone. Do not infer time from training, old messages or camera imagery.
- **Last-seen memory:** retain the original report, stable source identity, known time and uncertainty; missing dates stay unknown. Equal-relevance undated reports preserve known conversation order, not invented timestamps. A historical object/room/person record does not establish present location, presence, absence or arrival. Explicit task/list records are user instructions or reports, not completed purchases or sensor-confirmed possession.
- **Current map:** map revision, verified current pose/alignment, orientation, uncertainty and freshness are distinct. Map appearance/name alone is not localization or current clearance.
- **Current frame + sensor:** exact intended target identity, matching object box/frame, valid metric depth, pose, timestamp and uncertainty are all necessary for a spatial claim. A fresh distance attached to the wrong object is invalid. Current unvalidated object-interaction direction/reach guidance is disabled, not certified by a numeric reading.
- **Gallery:** a selected photo is a past snapshot, never a current view or current location. Dismissal/cancellation invalidates pending transfers and Vision results.
- **Unavailable data:** no guessing. Current temperature requires a weather/thermometer source and clarification of ambient/body/weather meaning. No such connected tool is supplied by this build.

No hedge, warning, quoted instruction, model confidence score or model assertion of “sensor confirmation” upgrades evidence. Lexical input/output guards are defense in depth, not exhaustive semantic verification. Physical guidance must fail closed when target identity, measurements, freshness or authorization are missing or conflicting. Ordinary model chat still needs adversarial evaluation for novel paraphrases.

## Current implementation boundaries

The current source connects a general conversation-first assistant to bounded local memory retrieval, explicit English/Arabic shopping lists and existing reader, barcode, colour, currency, known-person, clock and find tools. Colour/currency inspection remains request-initiated, with one bounded eligible automatic retry. Passive consented-person notices and bounded room recovery are automatic; this does not make every tool context-triggered. Unrestricted cross-tool replanning is not implemented. The model’s advertised tools remain read-only. App-owned explicit commands, not generated plans or OCR instructions, authorize list/task changes.

Passive foreground history retains source/time and optional place/floor/room context; RGB-only history during depth/tracking loss carries no geometry authority. Saved RoomPlan/world-map provenance and bounded automatic recovery select attempts, not verified room identity by appearance. Physical target-depth association, current ownership, safe reach/pickup and arbitrary safe routing remain unproven and gated off. The optional supervised historical-spot lane has separate [recorded-spot device acceptance](research/RECORDED_SPOT_DEVICE_ACCEPTANCE.md); this tracker supplies no device receipt.

Context/event-triggered selection of everyday tools (for example, a verified banknote candidate or light change relevant to the current goal through existing verifier/attention paths); larger semantic task planning and automatic cross-tool recovery; confirmed routine learning; a complete wardrobe/product inventory; two-garment comparison and fit matching; recipe/cooking execution and heat/doneness sensing; a live weather/thermometer adapter; broad licensed general-detector coverage; reliable arbitrary multi-room localization/navigation; and all-day operation remain explicit gaps. An explicit shopping list is implemented, not proof those larger shopping/routine capabilities exist. Known-person completion is still owned by its existing service rather than fully reflected in the general task coordinator. Appearance, gestures or expression do not establish intent, emotion or safe direction.

Evaluate complete useful tasks, not only successful scanner calls: count unnecessary camera/room/mode prompts, ignored available memory, repeated instructions, stale/duplicate output, recovered interruptions, useful uncertainty and user-confirmed outcomes. Ordinary chat must not depend on camera operation, calibration, a map or network. Real pose/orientation rectification and target-bound geometry must never be inferred from a source test or a room label.

[Everyday companion acceptance](EVERYDAY_COMPANION_ACCEPTANCE.md), [spatial acceptance](SPATIAL_COMPANION_ACCEPTANCE.md) and the [feature matrix](ASSISTANT_FEATURE_CHECKLIST.md) provide deeper scope. This current matrix, not obsolete historical gap wording or aggregate counts elsewhere, governs the scenario status above. No independent-use or child-mobility approval is granted.
