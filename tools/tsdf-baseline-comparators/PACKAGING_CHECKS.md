# Packaging-only checks

The initial public-path scanner matched its own literal forbidden prefix inside the newly authored validator. The validator pattern was represented as byte-string pieces, then the packet manifest and validation were regenerated. No scientific source, result, numeric value, historical hash or private original changed. This was a packaging-scanner self-match, not a failed scientific evaluation.

Thirteen synthetic byte restoration fixtures pass, including wrong hashes, traversal, symlinks, duplicate/order errors, size limits and refusal to overwrite outputs/partials. These tests do not load PLY geometry or decode a recorded array. The full real parts are independently streamed through SHA-256 by the validator without producing duplicate full PLY files.
