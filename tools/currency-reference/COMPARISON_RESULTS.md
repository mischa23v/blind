# Historical comparison, recovered narrative only

**Do not attribute these observations to the reconstructed implementation.** No real-image tests were repeated during recovery. Original private images, immutable reports, manifests and receipts are missing. Counts below are retained from earlier conversation; they are not a reconstructed evidence dataset.

The original development comparison used SIFT, ORB and AKAZE, frozen experimental thresholds and no new classifier training. Nine references covered historical 1; paper/polymer 5; front/back 10; and front-only 50, 100, 200 and 500. There was no 20, modern 1 or complete issue/side coverage. Seventeen source-query cases included mostly-full fronts, partial/folded and unsupported-side queries, a historical front/back composite, one USD image and an apple image. The flat/folded 100 images depicted the same physical note. Transformations, reverse directions and repeated testing were not independent trials.

With original reference bytes, each method accepted one permitted single-note case and one unsafe composite, with 15 abstentions. With separately prepared reference boundaries, each accepted three permitted single-note cases and the same unsafe composite, with 13 abstentions. No wrong denomination or USD/apple acceptance was observed in those small diagnostic runs. This is not a population accuracy estimate or proof of unknown rejection.

SIFT and AKAZE accepted 100, paper 5 and 10. ORB accepted 100, paper 5 and polymer 5, but the polymer query selected the paper-5 reference. That was correct denomination evidence, not material discrimination. All methods accepted the known historical-1 front within a two-region front/back composite, demonstrating failure to establish a single-note scene. Correct label did not mean correct overall decision.

AKAZE was provisionally selected for a disabled candidate-evidence path because its accepted cases equalled SIFT's in that development sample and it cost less in one cold desktop comparison. Historical total times for 17 prepared-reference calls were SIFT 29.18s, ORB 5.74s and AKAZE 9.87s, including rebuilding reference descriptors. These are neither recovered benchmarks nor iPhone latency measurements. No method was a release/safety winner.

The historical AlFloos dataset record cited https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/gfbati/alfloos, uploader Ghassan Bati and displayed GPL 3 dataset licensing, with exact source paths/hashes recorded privately at the time. Underlying banknote art rights were separate; no production activation or redistribution was authorized by those diagnostics. That detailed source record is now missing. Historical/negative Commons records are likewise unavailable in this recovered package. Do not fabricate them or treat this narrative as a licensed asset inventory.

The 500 query had been EXIF-normalized and resized with a receipt, not counted as another independent image. Later inspection identified edge clipping/curvature; its abstention was not evidence of failure on a fully visible flat note. Reference preprocessing used approximate assistant-reviewed visible boundaries and remained development, not human-audited release data. Further retained failure details are in `FAILURE_BREAKDOWN.md`.
