Phone
iPhone 17 Pro or newer (Pro or Pro Max only). Apple has kept LiDAR off its non-Pro iPhones, and it has appeared on every Pro and Pro Max through the iPhone 17 Pro. Android phones don't have reliable LiDAR, so do Android later.

You'll also need a Mac (Xcode only runs on macOS) and an Apple Developer account.

Maps
IMDF (Apple's indoor map format, an open standard) or OSM Simple Indoor Tagging for mall maps. Ask your friend which format their LiDAR data exports to.
RTAB-Map (BSD license) for large venue mapping and positioning later.
Open source to build on
Soundscape (community version). Microsoft Research built it around 3D audio cues that help people build a mental map and feel more comfortable in unfamiliar spaces. Microsoft released the code on GitHub, and a community effort has filled in missing pieces to keep it working. It's iOS-only, like your plan. Fork it for spatial audio and outdoor awareness instead of building those from scratch.
RTAB-Map for SLAM and relocalization in big venues.
License warnings
ORB-SLAM3 is GPLv3. Avoid it for a commercial product.
Ultralytics YOLO is AGPL-3.0. Commercial use needs their paid license. Alternatives: YOLOX or RT-DETR (Apache-2.0), or Apple's built-in detectors.
