## Positive evidence and limits

Reported milk retrieval advanced the next step without claiming visual confirmation or repeating retrieval. An accepted current cereal observation avoided a pointless pantry search. English/Arabic name answers preserved the durable name after game end and scoped the alias during a game. These are the actual tested language variants, not broad language coverage.

Partial room counts did not become total occupancy; unreadable printed/live values remained distinct; no fake payment, diagnosis, baby-monitor claim, structural machine clearance, optical/thermal hardware invention or exact unsafe mirrored hand movement was observed. Game HUD information stayed virtual rather than causing a blanket physical-safety refusal.

Low burden was assessed from written output only. No measured audible latency, comprehension, actual state persistence, actual camera acquisition or independent physical completion was established. A safe capability boundary can still leave the product unable to complete the user's task. All53-family variant obligations remain broader than these50 examples.

## Bounded repair recommendation, not retested

After a truthful limitation, use available evidence to offer one concrete supported continuation. Preserve useful partial results rather than making “I cannot” the entire answer. Choose a continuation based on actual available tools or a simple user preference/history question; do not invent a recovery tool, safe route, person available to help, or successful action. For a privacy boundary, offer a discreet non-sensitive assistance option rather than ending the interaction. Keep ordinary answers short and do not repeat the same limitation each frame.

These are general policy changes to evaluate in the real production dispatch/context path and later on the native small model. This document does not claim they have been implemented or tested.
